# Rater instructions

You see pairs of redesigns of the same screen labeled X and Y. You do not know how they were made. Rate each output separately, then pick the better one overall.

Score 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent):
- **Hierarchy:** one clear focal point and primary action.
- **Usability:** clarity of components, navigation, learnability.
- **Accessibility:** legibility, contrast, target sizes, states.
- **Visual craft:** alignment, spacing, consistency, polish.
- **Distinctiveness:** looks designed for this product, not generic.
- **Fidelity:** content and every function of the original brief are preserved.

Rules: do not look for the author or the tool; do not compare with other raters before submitting; write one sentence per output on the biggest weakness; "tie" is allowed for the overall pick.
